04 / Service Workflow

A structured way to move from symptom to verified operation.

STEP 01

Inspect

Understand the machine, fault condition, environment and production impact.

STEP 02

Diagnose

Isolate mechanical, electrical, electronic or process-related root causes.

STEP 03

Repair

Correct the fault using service procedures, parts and component-level work where applicable.

STEP 04

Validate

Calibrate, test parameters and verify reliable operation under working conditions.

STEP 05

Document

Record service actions, findings and recommendations for traceability and follow-up.
